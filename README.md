# Car-sales-project
This project provides an in-depth analysis of car sales data to understand market behavior, seasonal trends, model performance, and forecasting insights. Using data analytics techniques, the project examines how sales vary across regions, time periods, and customer segments, helping businesses make informed decisions on marketing, production.

- Project Objectives:
1️.Market Analysis

Study overall sales behavior and regional variations.

Identify high-demand car models and manufacturers.

Understand customer preferences and demographic patterns.

2️.Seasonal Patterns & Competitor Analysis

Detect cyclical & seasonal sales trends.

Compare performance across brands and models.

Identify peak sales periods for marketing and promotional strategies.

3️.Forecasting & Predictive Analytics

Use historical data to predict future car sales.

Support business decisions related to budgeting, advertising, product launches, and investment.

Provide forecast accuracy measures and future trend curves.

4️.Supply Chain & Inventory Optimization

Optimize dealership stock levels.

Prevent overproduction and understock issues.

Improve logistics and inventory turnover using data-driven insights.

- Tools & Technologies Used:

Python (Pandas, NumPy, Matplotlib, Seaborn)

Jupyter Notebook

Time-Series Forecasting (ARIMA, Prophet, or ML models)

Excel / CSV Data

Data Visualization Libraries

-Key Insights Delivered:

Regional and model-wise performance highlights

Seasonal and monthly sales patterns

Competitor brand comparison

Demand forecasting for upcoming sales cycles

Inventory optimization recommendations
